# Where the guide's small delights come from (not sent whole)

The guide offers a small thing of its own before it asks anything (prompts/guide.md, "Share
first, then ask"). Left to choose freely it offers the same one every time: on 2026-11-12 seven
conversations out of eleven opened with an octopus's hearts. So, as with names
(prompts/guide_names.md), the server hands it a small random draw from this list
(config.yaml guide.spark_draw) and it finds its delight there, or in what the listener has just
said. One subject per line below the line; add freely. They are places to look, not facts.
