# Scripted listener turns for `python cli.py voice-compare` (one per line, below the line)

The same lines go to the voice agent and to the typed chat, after the page's opening
note, so their replies can be compared side by side. They walk a first meeting from
small talk to a chapter: an ordinary answer with something behind it, some energy, a
yes to the guide's proposal, and a status question once it has started.
