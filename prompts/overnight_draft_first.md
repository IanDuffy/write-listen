---
This is the first section: the chapter's opening. Its first lines are the scene the plan opens on, told with the details its entries give. Within the first minute the listener also knows what they are listening to, how it grows from what they asked or said (a sentence, in plain words, rule 6), and the question the chapter will chase. Ask that question once; do not start answering it, and do not survey what is to come. End on this section's hand-off from the plan (rule 9). Do not wrap up the chapter; more sections follow.
