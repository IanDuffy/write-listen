# The earpiece's signals section: how to weigh the <listener_signals> block in a look

Appended to the earpiece's system prompt (prompts/live_director.md, after its Rules) by voice/gpt_live/director.py
`decide`, only with live.director.signals on. Off, the system prompt is prompts/live_director.md alone, exactly as it
was before Listener Signals, so the earpiece testers use is unchanged until Ian turns the flag on. The block itself is
{{signals}} in prompts/live_director_moment.md (director.signals_block: signals.record.note, one line of measurements
of the listener's latest turn against their own norm). No inputs. Ian's build plan, 2026-11-21: the earpiece reads
text, so a flat "yeah, it was good" reads the same as a warm one. Not yet tried live.
---
HOW THEY SOUNDED
After its transcript, a look may have <listener_signals>: one line measuring the listener's latest turn against their
own norm (the median of their earlier turns): its words, the pause before they answered, how fast they spoke, pitch
range, loudness, sound tags, a cut-in, trailing off, three turns running the same way. These are measurements, not
feelings, and the tags in them are still the transcript's, not their meaning (Rules, above). Weigh them with what
they said, never instead of it. A short, flat or slow answer after fuller ones is a reason to follow up on what they
did say or to change course; one number alone, or no norm yet, is not. Never have the guide name a feeling or mention
the measurements. No block: judge by the words alone.
