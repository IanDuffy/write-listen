`## <key>` names a persona's `visit:` (prompts/interviewer_testers.md). The text is what demo/debrief.visit_brief would

## sailor
WHAT THEY ASKED FOR
- They asked for a piece about: how sails can go upwind
- In their words: "I always wondered how a sailboat can go against the wind."

WHAT THEY SAID TO THE VOICE (their own words, oldest first)
- "Hi. I just got back from sailing on the bay."
- "I race on weekends, small boats, nothing fancy."
- "I always wondered how a sailboat can go against the wind."

THE PIECE
- Title: "Sailing Into the Wind"
- Part 1, "The Wing on the Water" (heard): A sail is a wing: it is pulled by lift, not pushed.
- Part 2, "The Keel's Half" (heard): Without the keel the boat would slide sideways; the two together go upwind.
- Part 3, "Tacking" (not heard): Why boats zigzag, and what that costs a racer.

THEIR QUESTIONS WHILE IT WAS READ
- They asked: "Wait, so is it like an airplane wing?" The voice answered: "Very like one: the curve makes the air on one side move faster, and the sail is pulled toward it."

## commuter
WHAT THEY ASKED FOR
- They asked for a piece about: the history of coffee
- In their words: "Something about the history of coffee? I have a 25 minute drive."
- Why it mattered to them: something to learn on the way to work

WHAT THEY SAID TO THE VOICE (their own words, oldest first)
- "Morning! Just about to head out."
- "Something about the history of coffee? I have a 25 minute drive."

THE PIECE
- Title: "The Bean That Woke the World"
- Part 1, "A Goat Story" (heard): The legend of Kaldi and what we actually know about coffee's start in Ethiopia.
- Part 2, "Penny Universities" (heard): London's coffee houses, where a penny bought a seat in the argument.
- Part 3, "The Morning Cup" (heard): How coffee became the start of the working day.
